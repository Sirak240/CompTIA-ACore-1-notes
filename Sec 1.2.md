The CIA Triad//
This is an easy way to remember i.t. security. The (C) is for -prevent discosure of information to unauthorized individuals or systems. The (I) is messages can not be modified without detection. (A) means systems and network must be up and running.

Integrity//
Hashing can be used to provide integrity. If your hash matches the senders hash then its the same as it was sent.
Digital signatures would be used when sending data there for it takes a hash and encryption algorithm, this conforms the person sent the data

Non- repudiation//
Cryptography has important foundations such as insuring when an individual sends data to a third party. The party has to then verify the information if it came from the original sender. you'd have to make sure it has a digital signature.
